# Skills

One section per skill, added as skills come up. Each section records: name, rarity (white, gold, unique, inherited unique), SP cost, trigger conditions, effect and values, duration, and the source with its date. Then come notes on interactions: what it stacks with, what it conflicts with, and the courses or strategies where it fires reliably or wastes itself.

Group the sections by effect type: velocity, acceleration, recovery, positioning and lane, debuffs, and passive or green.
