# Game mechanics

Sourced notes on how the game works. Cover how each stat affects races, aptitude penalties, mood, energy and failure rate, friendship training, hints, the inheritance and spark rules, and each scenario's own mechanics. Cite a source and a date for each section, and note which server it applies to.

## Skill costs

Source: uma.guide glossary ("Fast Learner", in `knowledge/data/glossary.json`) and skills guide (https://uma.guide/guides/skills), read 2026-10-18. Global.

- **Hints** take 10/20/30/35/40% off a skill's base SP at hint levels 1–5.
- **Fast Learner** (Sharp, Kiremono) is a career condition that "decreases the cost of all Skills by 10% of its base cost". Read as 10% of the base price, so it adds to the hint discount (Lv3 + Fast Learner = 40% off). The planner's Step 5 has a Fast Learner box that does this.
- A **gold (or ◎) skill** needs its white (or ○) bought first; the white's price counts toward the gold.

## Sparks and inheritance

Source: uma.guide, Sparks & Inheritance (https://uma.guide/guides/sparks), read 2026-10-18. Global.

- Sparks activate three times in a career: at the start, and at the two Inspiration events (the beginning of April in the Classic and Senior years).
- **White skill sparks** pass on their skill only by chance. Base rates per activation at 0 affinity: 3% for a 1★ white skill spark, 6% for 2★, 9% for 3★; the odds are multiplied by (1 + affinity / 100). Parents proc more often than grandparents.
- Race sparks give stats, or stats and a skill; scenario sparks give stats.
- Green (unique) sparks: a parent's inherited unique is acquired at the start of the career; a grandparent's only from inspiration (5/10/15% base for 1–3★).
- **What umaLord does with it:** skills from the plan's parents' white sparks (account/parents.md) are listed in Step 5 with "only if a parent's spark passes it on", and auto-pick buys them only when pinned, because a spark may never proc.
